\section{Future Research}
\label{sec:future-research}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Repeat experiments on a cloud-managed Kubernetes service (GKE, EKS, or AKS) with multi-node clusters to validate whether the crossover point shifts in a production-grade environment.
  \item Test with real production workloads---data ingestion, transformation, and analytics pipelines---instead of synthetic CPU-bound workloads, to capture the effect of I/O-bound and memory-intensive job profiles.
  \item Compare crossover points across different workflow orchestration frameworks (Airflow, Prefect, Argo Workflows) to determine whether the crossover is framework-specific or architectural.
  \item Investigate the Horizontal Pod Autoscaler's impact on crossover behaviour in dedicated scaling experiments.
  \item Extend the concurrency range beyond L6 (10 jobs) to identify behaviour at higher workload scales.
\end{enumerate}
