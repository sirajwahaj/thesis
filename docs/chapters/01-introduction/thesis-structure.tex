\section{Thesis Structure}
\label{sec:thesis-structure}

Chapter~\ref{ch:literature-review} reviews the literature across three pillars. Chapter~\ref{ch:method} defines the method, infrastructure, experimental design, tools, and metrics. Chapter~\ref{ch:results} presents the results of all three experiments. Chapter~\ref{ch:discussion} discusses the findings in relation to the research questions and the crossover point. Chapter~\ref{ch:conclusions} presents conclusions, practical recommendations, and directions for future research.
