\section{Practical Value and Target Audience}
\label{sec:practical-value}

These findings give small teams, startups, and engineers a data-based answer to: \emph{at what workload scale does migrating a workflow orchestration system from a VM to Kubernetes become worth the added complexity and overhead?}

The practical value is grounded in direct experience. During the author's internship at a small analytics company ($\sim$10 employees), the team migrated Dagster from VM-based Docker containers to Kubernetes on GKE. The migration improved operational capabilities---branch-based deployments, pod-level isolation, and automated scaling---but introduced new complexity in configuration, debugging, and scheduling overhead. The team lacked empirical data to evaluate whether the migration was justified for their specific workload scale. This thesis provides exactly that kind of data: measured thresholds and crossover points that inform the migration decision rather than leaving it to assumption.

The target audience includes DevOps engineers, platform teams, and technical managers who operate pipeline orchestration systems and face the decision of when---or whether---to migrate from VM-based to Kubernetes-based execution.
