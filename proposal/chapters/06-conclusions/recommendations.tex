\section{Practical Recommendations}

Based on the experimental findings, the following recommendations are offered for
engineering teams making \vm{}-to-Kubernetes migration decisions for workflow orchestration
systems:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textit{[To be completed based on the actual crossover point finding.]}
  \item Measure execution time variance, not just mean performance, when evaluating system
        stability --- variance is the primary signal that a system is under contention
        stress.
  \item Use version-controlled infrastructure (Helm charts, \textsc{iac}) to ensure the
        experimental environment can be reproduced and the migration can be rolled back.
  \item Do not migrate to Kubernetes at low concurrency levels --- the overhead cost
        exceeds the isolation benefit until the \vm{} approaches its contention threshold.
\end{enumerate}
