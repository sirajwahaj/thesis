\section{Practical Value and Target Audience}

These findings give small teams, startups, and engineers a data-based answer to the
question: \textit{at what workload scale does migrating a workflow orchestration system from
a \vm{} to Kubernetes become worth the added complexity and overhead?}

The practical origin of this question comes from the author's internship at Insighta Inc.,
a small analytics company that needed to decide whether to migrate their \dagster{} data
platform to Kubernetes. The decision was made without empirical data. This thesis provides
the kind of quantitative evidence that such decisions should rest on.

The results are most directly applicable to small and medium-sized engineering teams who
run pipeline orchestration workloads on self-managed infrastructure. The controlled
experimental design allows the findings to be replicated on comparable local or cloud
infrastructure.
