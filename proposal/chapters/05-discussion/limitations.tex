\section{Limitations}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Environments differ in scheduling mechanisms and networking --- treated as part of
        the behavioural comparison, not eliminated
  \item Single physical host --- results may not generalise to multi-node or cloud-hosted
        infrastructure
  \item Synthetic workloads --- may not capture all real-world pipeline behaviour patterns
  \item \dagster{} as representative system --- other workflow frameworks may behave
        differently
  \item No autoscaling measured --- the single-node Kind setup eliminates node-level
        scaling; results characterise fixed-resource behaviour only
\end{itemize}
