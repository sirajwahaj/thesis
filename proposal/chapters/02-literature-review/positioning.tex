\section{Positioning Statement --- The Gap This Thesis Fills}

Each of the four pillars above has been studied independently. Pillar~1 establishes
contention theory. Pillar~2 measures Kubernetes overhead at infrastructure level. Pillar~3
documents autoscaling response behaviour. Pillar~4 provides the theoretical basis for pod
isolation benefits.

What is missing is a study that connects these four pillars at the \textit{application-level
workflow execution layer} under controlled concurrent workload conditions. This thesis
occupies that intersection, measuring how infrastructure-level mechanisms produce
application-level behavioural outcomes for workflow orchestration systems under load, and
identifying the specific workload threshold at which the architectural migration produces a
net benefit.
