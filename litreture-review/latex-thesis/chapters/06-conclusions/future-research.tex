\section{Future Research}
\label{sec:future-research}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Repeat experiments on a cloud provider (GKE, EKS, or AKS) to validate whether the crossover point shifts in a managed multi-node environment.
  \item Test with real production workloads---data ingestion, transformation, and model inference pipelines---instead of synthetic workloads.
  \item Compare crossover points across different workflow orchestration frameworks (Airflow, Prefect, Argo Workflows).
\end{enumerate}
