\section{The Ask}

\subsection{What is being built}

A 10-week MVP sprint (solo founder) to ship Runway v0.1 and acquire the first 5 paying pilots.
Budget is zero — the MVP runs locally on developer hardware (M2 MacBook, 16GB RAM).

\subsection{Target milestones}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Week 10:} MVP shipped, 5 pilot users onboarded, first Slack alert sent
  \item \textbf{Month 4:} 3 documented accurate predictions, first paying customer
  \item \textbf{Month 8:} \texteuro{}2k MRR, Airflow support live, 30 customers
  \item \textbf{Month 18:} \texteuro{}10k MRR, YC or similar accelerator application
\end{itemize}

\vspace{8pt}

\begin{insight}
\textbf{The window is now.} The data observability market is consolidating around
enterprise players (Monte Carlo, Bigeye). The SMB segment — small data teams who cannot
afford \$1,000/month tools — is underserved and growing. Runway is the right product
at the right price at the right time.
\end{insight}
