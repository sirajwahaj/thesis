\subsection{Why now}

Three converging trends create the window:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Dagster adoption accelerating.} Dagster crossed 10,000 GitHub stars in 2024
        and is displacing Airflow at data-engineering-led companies. It has no native predictive
        monitoring tooling.

  \item \textbf{Data teams are shrinking their infra budgets.} After 2023--2024 layoffs, small
        data teams (3--8 engineers) are expected to maintain the same pipeline SLAs with fewer
        people. They need automation, not dashboards.

  \item \textbf{Kubernetes adoption in data engineering is reaching the early majority.}
        Teams know they \emph{should} migrate but lack the data to justify the decision to
        their CTO. Runway gives them that data.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Competitive differentiation}

\begin{insight}
\textbf{Runway's moat is the crossover methodology}: an empirically validated,
published framework (Wahaj, 2026) that maps pipeline stress signals to infrastructure
decisions. This cannot be replicated without the underlying experimental data.
It is defensible as a trade secret and potentially as a patent on the predictive
infrastructure recommendation algorithm.
\end{insight}
