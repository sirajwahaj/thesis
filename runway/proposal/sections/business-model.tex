\subsection{Go-to-market strategy}

\textbf{Phase 1 — Community (Months 1--4):}
Publish the Dagster sensor as open source on GitHub. Write one technical post on the
predictive methodology. Target the Dagster community Slack (8,000+ members) and the
Data Engineering Weekly newsletter (25,000 subscribers).
Goal: 200 installs, 5 paying pilots.

\vspace{4pt}

\textbf{Phase 2 — Referral (Months 5--8):}
Each prediction that proves accurate is a case study. Data engineers share their war stories.
Build a public prediction accuracy leaderboard to create social proof.
Goal: 30 paying customers, first \texteuro{}2k MRR.

\vspace{4pt}

\textbf{Phase 3 — Partnerships (Months 9--18):}
Pursue listing on Dagster's official integrations page and AWS Marketplace.
Target data engineering consultancies as channel partners (they recommend tools to every client).
Goal: 120 customers, \texteuro{}10k MRR.

\subsection{Why this pricing works}

\begin{insight}
\textbf{One prevented production failure pays for 12 months of Pro.}
A single pipeline outage costs a B2B SaaS company an estimated \texteuro{}3,000--15,000
in engineering time, customer impact, and SLA penalties. Runway's value is not
``nice monitoring'' — it is insurance against a concrete, recurring cost.
\end{insight}
