\section{Risks \& Mitigations}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{0.96\textwidth}{l X X}
  \toprule
  \textbf{Risk} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Mitigation} \\
  \midrule
  Prediction miscalibration
    & A wrong breach date destroys trust. One bad prediction can lose a customer permanently.
    & Ship confidence intervals, not point estimates. Log every prediction.
      Surface accuracy retrospectives to users. \\[4pt]
  Small initial TAM
    & Dagster is growing but still smaller than Airflow.
    & Airflow support in v1.1 expands TAM \texttimes{}3 with minimal code change.
      Architecture is framework-agnostic. \\[4pt]
  Larger tool adds feature
    & Monte Carlo or Datadog could add latency trend prediction.
    & They target enterprise (\$1k+/mo). Runway's moat is price, Dagster-nativeness,
      and the crossover methodology — hard to replicate without the underlying research. \\[4pt]
  Over-engineering
    & Academic instinct to build the full vision before validating.
    & Strict MVP discipline: no dashboard, no LLM, no billing until 5 paying pilots confirm demand. \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
