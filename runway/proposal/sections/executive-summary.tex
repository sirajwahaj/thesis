\section{Executive Summary}

\begin{insight}
\textbf{Runway} monitors data engineering pipelines, detects early stress signals, and predicts
exactly when a failure will occur — days or weeks in advance. It then recommends the concrete
infrastructure action that prevents the failure. No dashboard to configure. No rules to write.
One Slack message with a date and a decision.
\end{insight}
